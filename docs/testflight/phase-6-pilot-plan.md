# Phase 6 TestFlight pilot plan

## Objective and cohort

Run a two-week MVP pilot with 30–50 qualified independent dog trainers. Recruit across iPhone and iPad, German and English, group and individual training, and stores with 5, 50, and up to 200 active dogs. Demo fixtures use fictional names and contain no real client data.

## Before invitation

- Complete the iPhone/iPad test matrix at minimum OS and current OS.
- Check Dark Mode, increased contrast, VoiceOver, and XXXL Dynamic Type.
- Verify airplane mode, low-storage behavior, JSON/CSV backup, diagnostics export, archive/delete preview, and app lock.
- Provide a short privacy notice: all operational data stays local; exported backups may contain private notes; diagnostics contain no PII.

## Pilot cadence

- Day 0: onboarding and baseline workflow capture.
- Days 1–3: activation check—create a dog, exercise, template, and session.
- Days 4–10: repeated real-world session completion and report sharing.
- Day 7: structured midpoint interview and blocker triage.
- Days 11–14: backup/restore rehearsal, usability follow-up, and price/pilot continuation question.
- Day 14: closeout, anonymized synthesis, and Go/Pivot/No-Go memo.

## Feedback and diagnostics

Participants submit a structured form with task, expected result, actual result, reproducibility, device/OS, and severity. A privacy-safe diagnostics export may be attached voluntarily. Do not request screenshots or backups containing real client data unless a separate, explicit support consent and secure transfer process exists.

## Blocker triage

- P0: data loss, private data exposure, duplicate ledger redemption, or unrecoverable launch failure—stop pilot distribution immediately.
- P1: core flow cannot complete or export integrity fails—fix before adding testers.
- P2: workaround exists, but timing/accessibility target is missed—prioritize within pilot.
- P3: polish or non-core request—record for later prioritization.

## Go/No-Go evidence

Go requires no open P0/P1, full automated suite green, no duplicate redemptions, successful backup integrity checks, median completion at or below three minutes, and the Phase-0 commercial evidence gates. The pilot itself must still collect real activation, repetition, report-sharing, crash-free-session, accessibility, and continuation evidence; implementation completion does not manufacture those results.
