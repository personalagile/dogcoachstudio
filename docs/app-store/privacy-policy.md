# Privacy policy draft

DogCoach Studio is designed as a local-first application. Client, dog, training, package, and report data is stored on the user's device. Version 1 does not operate a DogCoach Studio account service, advertising network, analytics SDK, or customer portal.

The app can create user-initiated backups, reports, and privacy-safe diagnostic files. Full backups and reports may contain personal data selected or entered by the user; the user controls where those exports are saved or shared. Diagnostic exports contain timestamps and closed technical event codes, not names, contact information, notes, or report content.

Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple may process purchase and account information under Apple's own terms. DogCoach Studio receives verified entitlement information but does not receive payment-card details.

Optional device authentication uses Apple's LocalAuthentication framework. Biometric data is handled by the operating system and is not accessible to or stored by DogCoach Studio.

Users can archive records, inspect dependent data before permanent deletion, create an export first, and explicitly delete local data. Removing the app may also remove its local data unless the user has retained an export.

Before publication, the operator must add legal identity, contact address, effective date, applicable retention/support details, and hosted Privacy and Support URLs. This draft is a product-technical description, not legal advice.
